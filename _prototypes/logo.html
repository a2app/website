A2App · Identity

An A that becomes App.

The product's whole claim is a transformation — an agent's intent turning into a running application. The mark performs that claim rather than describing it: a single letterform resolves, steps aside, and the word assembles out of it.

A → App · written by one pen
01 / STATE

The A draws itself in one unbroken stroke, centred and large, then the crossbar lands. One letter, alone, at rest.

02 / DISSOLVE

It travels while it goes — shrinking and sliding into the exact spot the word's A occupies, then breaking into flecks as it arrives. It leaves a trail: two mint echoes of the A stacked to its left, softening as they recede — so the whole mark reads left to right, from where the letter came from through to the word it became.

03 / REBUILD

The word is written by one pen at one speed — every stroke's duration comes from its measured length. The connector branches off the first bowl at the exact point they meet, so the line runs on into the next p instead of restarting.

Colour

The ramp runs left to right across the word, so the colour change carries the same idea the mark does — agent at the A, app by the end. That part earns its keep. The question is how far the hue should travel.

Current — mint → indigo

78° of hue travel · saturation sags 35% mid-ramp, right on the connector

Corrected — mid-stop added

same endpoints, a saturated cyan pinned at 45% to hold the middle up

Shortened — mint → sky chosen

43° of travel · barely sags · steps off the mint-to-purple AI default

Flat — mint only

one colour · survives print, engraving, 16px and any one-colour context
